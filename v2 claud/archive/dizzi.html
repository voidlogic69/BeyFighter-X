DIZZI (DIZZARI)

Dizzi is a dub-exclusive character from the original Beyblade anime and Beyblade: V-Force. In the English dub framing, she is Kenny's Bit-Beast companion and appears through his computer rather than as a physical Beyblade in the stadium.

Her backstory says she ended up inside Kenny's computer, where she helps the Bladebreakers with analysis and information. Kenny is also known as “Chief” to the team.
